Our Process
Simple. Transparent. Results-Driven.
01

Intake & Role Brief

We conduct a deep-dive intake call to understand the role, team culture, compensation range, and success metrics before we ever source a candidate.

02

Active Search

We source passive and active candidates via LinkedIn Recruiter, Boolean search, and our professional network — targeting the right fit, not just the right resume.

03

Vetted Presentations

You receive 3–5 fully screened, briefed candidate profiles with written summaries. No spray and pray — only candidates we'd hire ourselves.

04

Offer & Close

We advise on offer construction, compensation benchmarking, and closing strategy — then follow up at 30, 60, and 90 days post-placement.
