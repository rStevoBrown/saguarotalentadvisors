About Us

A Recruiting Firm Built for Companies That Actually Need to Hire.

We're not a transactional resume-delivery service. We're a boutique talent advisory firm that takes your hiring seriously — because the wrong hire is expensive, and the right one changes everything.

Our Mission
A True Partner in Talent

Saguaro Talent Advisors was built on a simple premise: small and mid-size companies deserve the same quality of recruiting support that large enterprises have access to — without paying enterprise-level fees or getting lost in the queue of a national staffing firm.

We specialize exclusively in exempt professional-level placements. That focus means we understand the roles, the candidates, and the nuances that make a hire successful — not just on paper, but on the job.

A placement fee applies only when your new hire starts. A $1,500 kill fee applies if you cancel after 30 or more days of active search.

Who's Behind This
20+ Years in Recruiting
Fintech · SaaS · Enterprise · Staffing

Saguaro Talent Advisors was founded by a recruiting leader with over two decades of experience spanning contingency staffing, corporate talent acquisition, and executive-level recruiting strategy — across fintech, enterprise software, and professional services.

That career started on the agency side — running a division at a national staffing firm, managing 100+ client accounts across the Phoenix metro, building a team of staffing managers, and earning Presidents Club. The foundation built there: how to develop client relationships, source under pressure, and close competitive offers — still drives the approach today.

From there, ten years at a global fintech company meant building deep expertise in full-cycle technical and operations recruiting — filling 142 requisitions in a single year, leading large-scale engineering hiring surges, and saving hundreds of thousands in agency fees by building direct pipelines that actually worked. That was followed by managing technical recruiting teams at a global CRM software company and ultimately leading all of talent acquisition at a leading fintech, overseeing a 12-person team and reducing agency spend from $3M annually to near zero.

Saguaro exists because most small and mid-size companies don't have access to that level of recruiting expertise — and they're paying too much to firms that don't bring it. This is the boutique alternative: experienced, transparent, and built around your outcome.
