About Us

A Recruiting Firm Built for Companies That Actually Need to Hire.

We're not a transactional resume-delivery service. We're a boutique talent advisory firm that takes your hiring seriously, because the wrong hire is expensive, and the right one changes everything.

Our Mission
A True Partner in Talent

Saguaro Talent Advisors was built on a simple premise: small and mid-size companies deserve the same quality of recruiting support that large enterprises have access to, without paying enterprise-level fees or getting lost in the queue of a national staffing firm.

We specialize exclusively in exempt professional-level placements. That focus means we understand the roles, the candidates, and the nuances that make a hire successful, not just on paper but on the job.

A placement fee applies only when your new hire starts. A $1,500 kill fee applies if you cancel after 30 or more days of active search.

Who We Serve
Built for the Mid-Market

We work best with companies that have real hiring needs but limited internal recruiting resources, where the cost of a bad hire or a long vacancy is genuinely felt.

Company Size

50–500 employees. Large enough to need professional talent, lean enough that every hire matters.

HR Team Profile

Small or solo HR teams stretched across multiple functions. Recruiting is critical but not their only job.

Hiring Frequency

Occasional exempt-level openings, not ongoing high-volume hiring. You need a partner, not a contract staffing agency.

Role Types

Individual contributors to senior managers. Exempt professional roles across Finance, Operations, Technology, Healthcare, Legal, Marketing, and more.

Geography

Primary focus on West and Central US markets, with the flexibility to place nationally for the right role.

Budget Mindset

Value-conscious companies that want quality recruiting at a fee that makes business sense, not the most expensive option in the market.

Who's Behind This
Why I Started Saguaro
Fintech · SaaS · Enterprise · Staffing

I thought about starting my own business for 15 years before I finally took the leap. Over that time, I watched a lot of staffing firms overcharge their clients, and because of that, they never got repeat business.

My vision for Saguaro Talent Advisors is to build long-term relationships where I'm a trusted, honest recruiting advisor for the companies I work with. That's why my fees are substantially lower than other firms', and why I personally handle every search.

Before Saguaro, I recruited at Robert Half, spent a decade at PayPal, built a recruiting team at Zendesk, and most recently led talent acquisition for a national fintech company, where we cut agency spend from $3M to 1% of hires.
