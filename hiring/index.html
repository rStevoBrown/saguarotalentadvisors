Our Guarantee
60-Day Replacement Policy

We stand behind every placement. If a candidate we place voluntarily leaves or is terminated for cause within 60 days of their start date, we conduct one complimentary replacement search at no additional fee.

That guarantee reflects how seriously we take the quality of our submissions — and the confidence we have in our screening process.

Guarantee Conditions

✓Candidate voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause within 60 calendar days of start date
✓Client notifies Saguaro in writing within 5 business days of separation
✓Invoice has been paid in full (account in good standing)
✓Original position remains open and client actively engages in replacement search

Guarantee does not apply to layoffs, position eliminations, or role changes initiated by the client.
