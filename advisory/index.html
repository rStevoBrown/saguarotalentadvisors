Is This Right for You?
Signs You Need Advisory Support

Offers Keep Getting Declined

If candidates are accepting competitor offers or countering aggressively, your compensation structure may be out of market — and you don't know it yet.

Interviewers Aren't Aligned

When every manager interviews differently, you get inconsistent feedback, conflicting opinions, and slow decisions — all of which cost you good candidates.

Bad Hires Keep Happening

A pattern of short-tenure placements or underperforming hires usually points to a broken screening or evaluation process, not just bad luck.

No Formal Process Exists

If hiring is handled ad hoc — different process every time, no documented criteria, no structured scoring — you're operating on intuition alone.

Legal Exposure Concerns

If your managers have never been trained on what they can and cannot ask in an interview, you have real legal risk that one complaint can surface.

Growing Headcount Fast

Rapid growth breaks informal hiring processes. Before you scale from 30 to 100 people, you need the infrastructure to hire consistently and well.

Common Questions
FAQ
Can we do advisory services without using your recruiting?
Yes. Advisory services are completely standalone. Many clients engage us purely for interview training or compensation work, with no recruiting relationship required.
How many people can attend an interview training session?
Sessions are designed for up to 8 participants and work best in groups of 3–6. For larger teams, we recommend splitting into two sessions to maintain engagement and interactivity.
Is training delivered in-person or virtually?
Both options are available. Virtual sessions work well for geographically distributed teams. On-site sessions are available for clients in the West and Central US.
What does a compensation benchmarking project include?
We analyze market data for your target roles using current compensation sources, assess internal equity relative to existing staff, and deliver grade bands and pay ranges you can put into use immediately.
Do you offer bundled pricing for multiple services?
Yes. Combined interview training and compensation projects are available at a discounted rate. Clients who are also engaged in an active search may qualify for additional discounts.
